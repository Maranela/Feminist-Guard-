Privacy Policy

1. What is this?

Feminist Guard PRO is currently an MVP Prototype (test version) hosted on GitHub Pages. It is built with South Africa's POPIA privacy principles in mind.

2. Where is your data?

On your phone ONLY. Contacts, recordings, and name are saved in your browser's localStorage. We do NOT send it to any server, we do NOT sell it. If you clear your browser data, it is deleted.

3. Location?

Location is only used when YOU tap SOS. It creates a Google Maps link to share via YOUR WhatsApp. We do not track you.

4. POPIA Rights

You can: View, edit, delete your guardians anytime in Contacts → ✖. Delete all data: Chrome Settings → Clear Site Data.

5. Disclaimer

This MVP is NOT yet formally POPIA certified by a lawyer. Wording "Privacy First" means we designed for privacy, not that we have a certificate. For full certification, we will appoint an Information Officer after funding.

6. Contact

For data questions: Email - admin@feministguard.co.za (to be registered)

By using this prototype you understand it is for testing and feedback.
